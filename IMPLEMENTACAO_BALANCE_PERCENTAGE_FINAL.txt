================================================================================
 LOGICA DE CALCULO
================================================================================

profit_percentage = (total_profit / account_balance) * 100

Exemplos:
─────────
Balance: $1000  | Total Profit: $20  → (20/1000)*100 = +2.0%
Balance: $1000  | Total Profit: $10  → (10/1000)*100 = +1.0%
Balance: $1000  | Total Profit: $5   → (5/1000)*100 = +0.5%
Balance: $1000  | Total Profit: -$10 → (-10/1000)*100 = -1.0%
Balance: $1000  | Total Profit: $0   → (0/1000)*100 = 0.0%

Emoji Mapping:
──────────────
profit_percentage >= 1.0%  → 🔥 (muito bom!)
profit_percentage > 0%     → 📈 (positivo)
profit_percentage <= 0%    → 📉 (negativo ou zero)
